# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (Draft)

- Vercel deploys no longer fail with `unused_function`: the 60 s function time limit moves from `vercel.json` to `nuxt.config.ts` (`nitro.vercel.functions.maxDuration`), where Nitro's Vercel output picks it up.
- Media can live in a public Vercel Blob store instead of R2: setting `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` (and `BLOB_STORE_ID`) switches storage over, and `STORAGE_DRIVER` forces one. Direct uploads from the browser and the media processor now receive the headers to send along with the upload URL (`uploadHeaders`, `overlayUploadHeaders`, `putHeaders`); R2 sends none. The Etalase image policy follows the active store's host.
- `npm run build` now migrates the database after `nuxt build` (`npm run db:deploy`) and runs the seed by itself on a fresh database (no staff user yet); a live database is never re-seeded. `SKIP_DB_DEPLOY=1` builds without a database (CI preset builds, Docker images). `vercel.json` moves to the repo root with the per-minute tick cron, and only the `main` and `staging` branches deploy on Vercel.
- The public Etalase is redesigned: a header tinted with the creator's theme, bigger photo and social buttons, a search field with an icon, a product count, wider cards in three columns on desktop, and the shop shown as a coloured dot. Every Etalase now ends with a Socialz.id block (logo and a "Buat Etalase-mu sendiri, gratis" button to sign-up), on every plan: the "Tanpa tulisan dibuat dengan Socialz.id" plan feature is removed (migration 0014).
- Pasting a product link no longer saves it on its own: a preview card shows the shop's photo, title and price, and the creator adds it with Tambah (or Enter). When the preview finds a photo, the autofill saves that photo even if the shop page cannot be read again.
- Shops that hide their product page from us (Tokopedia) still get a product name, read from the link itself, and the creator can add the photo by hand: on the paste preview, on an empty product card in Link pendek, on the product page, and in the Etalase product sheet. The "Mengisi detail produk…" spinner now stops when the autofill is done instead of spinning forever.
- UX audit of every screen (phone and desktop screenshots, prioritised findings, a feedback and celebration system, drag and gesture proposals and a roadmap) in `docs/ux-audit/`. Documentation only; nothing in the app changed.
- Project foundation: Nuxt 4 app with the landing page, creator console (`/app`) and staff back-office (`/admin`) shells, in Bahasa Indonesia and English, with dark mode.
- Creators can sign up with email and password (or Google when configured), verify their email, sign in, reset their password and sign out. Every new creator gets a workspace with a 14-day Kreator trial.
- Staff can sign in to the back-office, which shows the sidebar their role allows.
- Background jobs run from a Postgres job table, triggered by a per-minute tick and immediate kicks, on any serverless host. `/healthz` reports database and tick health.
- Notification bell and notifications page for creators and staff, refreshed every minute.
- Back-office: settings editor (grouped by topic, kill switches ask for confirmation, test email), email template editor with live preview, notification template editor, kill-switch banner and a "Test environment" badge.
- The error page sends visitors back to the console they came from.
- Creators and staff can edit their profile (name, phone shown as 0812-…, photo resized in the browser) and change their password; Google-only creators can create one.
- Workspace settings (name, time zone, niche, affiliate programs), a one-click data export, and an account deletion request; the public /hapus-akun page explains the process in both languages.
- Settings has a Tampilan card: the light, dark or device theme (saved per device, the same choice as the user menu) and the app language (saved on the account, the same choice as on the profile page).
- Team: owners on plans with team access invite editors or viewers, manage roles, deactivate members; invitees accept from the emailed link. Plan-gated menu entries show a lock.
- Meta data deletion and deauthorize callbacks, with a public status page.
- The onboarding guide on Beranda can be hidden.
- Akun Sosial: connect Instagram (Business/Creator), Threads and Facebook Pages, pick which Pages to add, reconnect and disconnect; clear messages for personal Instagram accounts, plan limits and accounts already used elsewhere. TikTok, YouTube and X show "Segera hadir".
- Local development can run behind an https tunnel (set `NUXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to it, and the media storage behind a second hostname) so real Instagram, Threads and Facebook accounts can be connected and posts published.
- README with local development, the tunnel setup for real social accounts, deployment and troubleshooting. `npm run media:dev` now reads `.env` itself.
- After publishing, Instagram and Threads are the source of truth: the nightly sync (now 02:00 WIB) and a new "Sinkronkan" button (on a post and on the Terbit list) bring back caption edits made there, mark posts deleted there as "Dihapus di Instagram" (link hidden, history and last numbers kept) and update the numbers. The post detail shows when it was last synced and a history of what changed on the platform, with before/after captions; the list shows the latest edit. One deleted post no longer stops the numbers of the account's other posts.
- Tokens are stored encrypted and bound to their account; tokens near expiry refresh automatically every hour, and the owner is told when an account needs reconnecting.
- Media uploads go straight to storage; photos are converted to JPEG and videos (including iPhone HEVC) to H.264 MP4 ready for Reels, automatically.
- Plans: what a workspace may do follows its plan in force; a lapsed trial falls back to Gratis immediately. Limits show friendly messages, a quiet trial/grace banner sits under the top bar, an upgrade dialog opens only when a limit is hit, and the rail shows an Upgrade button when there is something to upgrade to.
- Back-office plan editor: prices, trial days, every limit (with "Tanpa batas") and every feature; plans in use or the default plan can't be deleted.
- Buat Postingan: write once for every connected account (all selected by default), upload photos and videos that are fixed automatically, see per-platform character counts and friendly hints (for example, Instagram links aren't clickable), pick a suggested time, then Jadwalkan, Posting sekarang or save a draft. Per-platform caption, format, first comment and alt text sit under "Opsi lanjutan".
- Postingan list with Terjadwal / Draf / Terbit / Gagal tabs, per-account status in plain words, "Coba lagi" on failures; post detail with reasons and fix buttons; duplicate, edit, cancel and delete.
- Hashtags in Buat Postingan: the caption's hashtags show as chips colored by quality (broken characters, numbers only, duplicates, too general, spam-bait, unrelated to the caption, over the limit), each with the reason and a remove button. "Saran tagar" suggests hashtags that fit the caption from a tag list staff manage at `/admin/hashtags`, checks hashtags the creator types, and saves reusable sets. "Riset" (paid, monthly allowance: Kreator 100, Pro unlimited) adds how each hashtag did on the creator's own posts and, once Meta approves it, how busy it is on Threads. One tap makes a Threads-only caption with 1 hashtag. Instagram's limit is now 5 hashtags per post, counting the first comment.
- Back-office fix: the FAQ "Tampilkan" switch no longer resets the question's category and order, and updates to pages, roles and menus keep every field they don't send (a role rename can't wipe its permissions, an edit can't republish a page).
- Kalender: month view with drag-to-reschedule on desktop and a day list on phones; tap a day to create a post for it.
- Posts publish automatically and reliably: a lost platform response is reconciled instead of posting twice, daily platform limits reschedule instead of failing, expired accounts pause their posts and ask for a reconnect, and the monthly plan allowance is shown before scheduling.
- Beranda shows upcoming posts and a fix-it alert for failed posts.
- Link Produk: paste a Shopee, Tokopedia, Lazada, TikTok Shop or other shop link and get a short link that counts clicks; the product name, photo and price fill in automatically. Copy, QR code, archive, and a per-product statistics page.
- "Tambah link produk" in Buat Postingan puts a product's short link in the caption; each post and platform gets its own link, so clicks show which post sold.
- Etalase: pick a name, and every product appears on one phone-first page for the bio link. Three themes, photo, short description, social links, categories, pin, labels, hide and reorder, a live preview, publish/hide, copy link and QR. The public page loads without any JavaScript, with search and category tabs.
- Statistik: total and unique clicks per day, top products, top posts, and clicks by source, platform, device and city, for 7, 30 or 90 days (limited to the plan's history, with a note when shortened).
- Clicks from bots and link previews are not counted; visitor IPs are never stored (daily salted hashes only).
- Balas Otomatis: when someone comments a keyword on an Instagram post, they get the product link by DM within seconds, plus an optional public reply picked at random from a few variations. Rules are set up in three steps (post, keywords, message + product) or from ready-made examples, with a "Coba" button that previews the DM without sending. An activity page shows who commented and whether the DM went out, with the reason in plain words when it didn't.
- Instagram's rules are enforced automatically: one DM per person, only for comments from the last 7 days, an hourly limit per account, an optional daily limit per rule, the plan's monthly DM allowance (the owner is told once when it runs out) and an emergency off switch for staff. Repeated notifications from Instagram never send a second DM.
- Paket & Pembayaran: monthly/yearly plan cards, pay through the Nexadream Payment Hub (QRIS, e-wallet, VA, card), continue or cancel an unfinished payment, payment history, cancel and resume a subscription. Choosing the same plan twice reuses the open charge, and switching plans cancels the old one first, so nobody pays twice. Returning from the payment page reads the payment's real status.
- Payments are only fulfilled after re-reading the status from the hub; callbacks for other platforms on the shared merchant are stored and ignored; a daily reconciliation picks up missed callbacks. An abandoned early renewal never starts the grace period.
- Renewals: reminders 7, 3 and 1 days before the plan ends; an unpaid period enters a grace period, then drops to Gratis. Trials end the same way. A downgrade never deletes anything: extra accounts, auto-reply rules and Etalase pages are paused, and the owner picks which accounts stay active.
- Back-office: payment gateway settings (keys stored encrypted, shown masked, one active gateway), payments, subscriptions and the payment callback log with replay.
- `scripts/mock-payment-hub.ts`: a local stand-in for the Payment Hub for development.
- Back-office: dashboard with platform health (publishing success per platform, accounts needing reconnection, auto-replies, tick and queue age), and reports per day/week/month (revenue, new paid, renewals, churn, trials lapsed, signups, posts, DMs, clicks, MRR and the current book).
- Back-office workspaces: search and filters, detail with usage against plan limits and a read-only support drill-down; suspend (publishing and auto-reply stop), reinstate (posts whose time passed fail instead of publishing late), plan override (recorded in subscription history, limits enforced) and delete (superadmin).
- Impersonation for support (superadmin): opens the creator console as the owner for one hour with a banner; payments are blocked and every action is recorded. "Berhenti menyamar" returns to the workspace page.
- Back-office people and access: creator logins (sign-in methods, revoke all sessions), staff invitations with an accept page, staff roles and deactivation, roles with a permission checklist, the permission catalog, and the sidebar menu editor (three levels, reorder).
- Back-office content and operations: CMS pages with a rich-text editor (HTML cleaned on save; privacy and terms always published), FAQ, contact messages, failed posts with safe retry, social account health with the kill switches, link and Etalase moderation (the owner is told why), the Meta webhook log with replay, the audit log and the job queue overview.
- Public site: a full landing page (hero with a product preview, features, three steps, supported platforms, pricing with monthly/yearly toggle, FAQ, contact form, final call to action) that reads plans, FAQ and company details from the database; a /harga page; /privacy and /terms from the CMS; a footer with company details, social links and legal pages; mobile menu; gentle reveal-on-scroll that respects reduced motion.
- Landing page for beginners, told in pictures instead of a diagram: a hero with a plain promise, price chips taken from the plans and a phone showing a post with what now happens by itself (scheduled, a comment answered in the DMs, Etalase clicks); "Kenal rasanya?", six illustrated everyday pains (staying up for the busy hour, a cropped product photo, #fyp with no views, not knowing which link got clicks, one bio link for many products, "mau kak" comments piling up), each naming the feature that fixes it; six matching feature illustrations of the real screens, including the photo and video editor and hashtag ideas; illustrated steps; a "Baru mulai? The check runs in the browser after load, so the page stays cacheable and signed-out visitors see no change.

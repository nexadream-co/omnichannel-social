# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (Draft)

- **Statistik shows more than clicks.** Each top number (Total klik, Pengunjung unik and the new **DM otomatis terkirim**) shows how it changed against the equally long period before (▲ 23%, ▼ 8%, "Baru"), but only when the plan's click history covers both periods. A new **Dari postinganmu** card says in one sentence how many of every 1.000 people who saw the period's posts clicked a product, compared with the period before. It only counts posts that have been live for a day, and only gives a number once they reach 100 views; Facebook, which gives no views yet, is left out with a note. A new **Balas Otomatis** card shows comments matched → DMs sent → clicks from DMs, plus the numbers per rule. Top posts now show their views and, when known, clicks per 1.000 views. The response type is shared by the page and the service (`shared/utils/analytics.ts`, `WorkspaceAnalytics`).
- Balas Otomatis DMs now get **their own short link per rule, product and post** (`dmShortLinkFor`) instead of one per product. A post's click count (post detail, Statistik) now includes the clicks from the DMs sent under it, so Instagram posts, whose captions have no clickable links, finally show what they sell. Links in DMs sent before this change keep working; their clicks are counted as "klik dari DM lama" without a rule. Migration 0018 adds `short_links.dm_rule_id` (no foreign key, so a deleted rule's links keep redirecting) and its unique index, narrows the caption link's unique index to `source = 'post'`, and adds an index on `post_targets (social_account_id, external_id)`. Caption links and "products in this post" ignore DM links.
- Buat Postingan shows **Simpan draf** as its own button next to **Jadwalkan** (it reads "Draf" on phones), instead of hiding it behind the ⋯ menu, which now holds only "Posting sekarang" and "Simpan sebagai ide". Leaving the composer with unsaved work (back button, a menu link) now asks with three answers: **Simpan draf** saves the post as a draft and goes on to where the creator was heading, **Tinggalkan** drops the changes, **Tetap di sini** stays. A draft that cannot be saved shows the reason and keeps the creator on the page. `useConfirmDialog` gains `choose()` for a dialog with a third answer, and `useUnsavedChanges` takes an optional `saveDraft`; other pages keep the two-button dialog.
- On the Kalender, the pink "add to this day" hover (tint, outline and +) now only shows on the empty part of a day. While the pointer is on a post or idea inside the day, the day stays plain and only the item shows its own hover. Two Tailwind variants in `main.css`, `day-hover` and `group-day-hover`, skip the effect while a `[data-day-item]` inside the day is hovered. Month and week views.
- The Pratinjau in Buat Postingan now shows every photo and video of a carousel and can be swiped like on the platform: touch swipe, mouse drag or trackpad, the arrow keys, or the arrow buttons (shown from tablet width up, hidden at the first and last item). The "1/3" counter and the dots follow the current item. Before, only the first item showed and the counter always read "1/N". Built on Nuxt UI's `UCarousel`. Other formats still preview only the first item, since that is all they publish.
- Buat Postingan's checks now say which platform and post type they apply to, and what that type accepts. For example: "Tambahkan minimal 2 foto atau video untuk Carousel Instagram", "Foto tidak bisa dipakai untuk Reels Facebook. Pakai video, atau ganti format di Opsi lanjutan", "Video terlalu panjang untuk Reels Facebook (maks 90 detik)". Before, they read "Tambahkan minimal 2 foto atau video." with no type, which was confusing when the post had been switched to Carousel. Caption, hashtag, mention and link limits name the platform too. `validateTarget` now sends `platform`, `format` and `media` (the kinds the format accepts) in each issue's params, and the composer shows them through the label maps. The uploader's "too many files" note has its own key, `channel.uploadLimit`.
- Tapping any day on the Kalender grid, from today on, opens a "Buat untuk {tanggal}" sheet with that date already set: **Buat postingan** opens the composer on that day, **Tulis ide** opens the idea editor dated that day, and ideas waiting in Bank Ide can be dropped on it with one tap. Before, the + only opened the composer straight away when Bank Ide was empty. Days that can be filled now show a pink tint, a thin pink outline and the + on hover (the + is always shown on touch screens); past days don't react. The sheet is a bottom drawer on phones and tablets.
- Kalender opens in the month view (Bulan) by default and remembers the last view the creator picked, Minggu or Bulan, on that device. The "Momen belanja" cards above the calendar are gone (`MomentStrip.vue` removed). Shopping moments still show as marks on their days and in the Beranda week card.
- The Nexadream Payment Hub can call back at `POST /api/payments/webhook` and send creators back to `/pay/done`, the addresses now registered with the hub for `socialz-iota.vercel.app`. The webhook uses the same receiver as `/api/webhooks/payments/nexadream_hub` (status always re-read from the hub). `/pay/done` forwards the order id to `/app/billing`, which shows the real payment status. The old addresses keep working.
- Kalender drag-and-drop now works with mouse, pen and touch (`useDragSource`, replacing the browser's native drag, which has no touch). Press and move a scheduled post or an idea, or on a phone hold it for a moment and then move it. A card with the post's picture follows the pointer with a pill naming the day and time it will land on, or "Tidak bisa ke tanggal lewat". Valid days get a dashed outline and past days fade. The page scrolls by itself near the top and bottom edge, and Esc cancels. The drop shows at once and is saved behind it: a refused save (for example an account that needs reconnecting) puts the post back with the reason, and "Urungkan" still works. On the phone agenda, a hold released in place still opens "Pindahkan ke…". Bank Ide cards drag the same way.
- Every post on the Kalender shows a brand-coloured badge per platform (Instagram gradient, Facebook blue, Threads/TikTok/X black, white in dark mode; `PlatformBadge.vue`) and the post's first photo or video poster, so posts are recognisable at a glance. The edge of each chip is coloured by status (scheduled, published, failed, overdue). The phone agenda also shows the target accounts (`@username`). `GET /api/app/posts/calendar` now returns `thumbnail` and `username` on each entry. The small grey platform icons in month-view day headers are gone, since the chips now carry the badges.
- The creator menu is shorter so the console reads as an app, not an admin panel: Beranda, Postingan, Link Produk, Etalase, Balas Otomatis, Statistik. **Postingan** gathers Kalender, Semua postingan and Galeri Media as tabs at the top of each (`PostsHubTabs.vue`); the pages and their addresses are unchanged, and the menu entry stays lit on all three. Akun Sosial and Tim move to the user menu (Bisnis group, Tim with a lock when the plan lacks it) and to the phone "Lainnya" sheet. The connected accounts are listed under the menu with their avatar and platform badge, a warning dot when one needs reconnecting, and a + to add one (`RailAccounts.vue`). Phone bottom nav: Beranda · Postingan · Buat · Link Produk · Lainnya.
- Social media logos across the console are in their own colours instead of grey (Instagram gradient, Facebook blue, YouTube red, WhatsApp green, Threads/TikTok/X black, white in dark mode; `PlatformIcon.vue`, `app/utils/brand.ts`): account chips and the preview tabs in Buat Postingan, Semua postingan, Beranda, Statistik, Akun Sosial and its picker, Balas Otomatis, the Etalase social fields and the landing page's "Bisa dipakai di" row. Account avatars on the post detail and account picker carry a platform badge. The public Etalase keeps the creator's theme colours.
